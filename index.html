Stress Management App

Problem Statement

Stress is a common issue in modern life, especially among students. Many people do not have simple tools to monitor and manage their mental health.

Objectives

  • Help users track their mood
  • Provide stress relief tips
  • Promote mental health awareness

Key Features

  • Mood Selection
  • Mood History
  • Daily Stress Tips

Technologies Used

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Netlify (Deployment), GitHub (Version Control)
